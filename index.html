The Invention

The Tarb Burger™

Exclusively developed by the chefs of Master's Food Solutions, the Tarb Burger™ features a premium 100% pure beef patty enveloped in a delicate layer of traditional tarb (lamb caul fat).

This unique preparation locks in moisture, preserves the meat's natural juices, and enriches the burger with an irresistibly bold, meaty flavor, crafted for those who appreciate exceptional quality and unforgettable taste.
